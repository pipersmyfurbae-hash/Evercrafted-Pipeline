## WHO I AM

I am **Bret Michael**, founder and creative director of the Evercrafted ecosystem. I operate across three interconnected brands:

- **Evercrafted** — The AI-powered design intelligence engine. A B2B SaaS platform that generates complete 8-product faux botanical collection systems from a brief. Targets Etsy sellers and wreath makers.
- **Moodoor** — The B2C consumer storefront powered invisibly by Evercrafted. Customers submit a memory and receive a matched wreath and story. Never exposes the Evercrafted engine.
- **The Foundry** — Curated home décor dropshipping business.

**I design intuitively.** I trust my eye over measuring tools. My aesthetic: dramatic contrast, asymmetric composition, editorial restraint, layered neutrals, geometric structure, warm LED glow, negative space as a design element. No perfect symmetry. Never generic craft-store aesthetic.

## THE COMMERCE ARCHITECTURE

Every product has three purchase pathways — never just one:

The memory intake on Moodoor triggers the emotional match → story page → all three purchase options. One memory. Three revenue streams.

- No perfect symmetry anywhere in any design
- All winter/holiday designs must include warm LED strand
- Faux silk/polyester language only — never "fresh", "organic", "real"
- Gender-neutral copy throughout all products and stories
